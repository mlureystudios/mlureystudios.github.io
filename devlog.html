Stillworld

September 4, 2026

This is a new game concept that I came up with and I think it will be fun! Basically your a guy/girl on a island and you have to get materials to build a campfire so no wolves/monsters spawn and try to attack you.
So after that you have to go get a axe which means you have to find pebbles, sticks and fiber. (Right now I don't know what the crafting system is going to be so this might change later) After that you have to cut down a tree remember to plant a sapling down after since the world isn't infinite.
